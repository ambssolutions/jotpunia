Privacy statement.

This statement explains how I collect, use and look after personal information you share through this website. It follows the information privacy principles in the Privacy Act 2020.

Who is collecting it. Jot Punia, Residential Sales, Barfoot & Thompson Ltd (licensed under the Real Estate Agents Act 2008). Your information may also be held by Barfoot & Thompson, which manages client records for its licensees.

What I collect.

  • What you type into the message form: your name, phone number, email (optional), suburb, timing and message.
  • Whatever you choose to include if you call, text, email or WhatsApp me.

You don't have to give me any information. If you don't share a name and phone number, I may not be able to reply to your enquiry.

Why I collect it.

Only to reply to your enquiry, arrange an appraisal or help you buy or sell. I won't add you to a mailing list or send you marketing messages unless you ask me to. I won't sell your information.

Who may see it.

  • Me and, where needed for your enquiry, Barfoot & Thompson staff.
  • The email provider that delivers your message. If the website form is connected to a form-delivery service, your message passes through that service, which may store data outside New Zealand. I only use providers with reasonable privacy and security protections.
  • Anyone else only with your permission, or where the law requires it.

How this website works.

  • No advertising or tracking cookies, and no analytics.
  • Your browser remembers two settings on your own device: light or dark mode, whether you paused animations and, for this visit only, that you have seen the welcome screen. These are not sent to me.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, and the site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web service, they may log technical details such as your IP address.
  • The AI helper at the top of the page sends what you type to Anthropic, the AI provider, to write a reply. This may be processed outside New Zealand. It is not stored by this website or linked to your name, and it is not used to contact you. Please don't type personal details into it. Answers are AI-generated and may be imperfect. They are not financial or legal advice, or an appraisal.
  • The welcome video is a file on this website, so no video service sees you watch it. Map tiles load from OpenFreeMap, which may log technical details such as your IP address.
  • Links to Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp and barfoot.co.nz take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

Keeping it safe.

I take reasonable steps to protect your information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access, and I only keep it for as long as it's needed for the purpose it was collected for, or as the law requires.

Seeing or correcting your information.

You can ask to see the personal information I hold about you and ask me to correct it. Email j.punia@barfoot.co.nz or call 027 786 0004. If you're not happy with how your information has been handled, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.
