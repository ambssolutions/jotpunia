# Welcome film

## What's live now
The site opens with a full-screen, 13-second title sequence: a montage of his listings, Jot revealed in front of his name with the Top Salesperson badge, his three advice points, and an end card. There are two versions: `assets/video/welcome-wide.*` for computers (16:9) and `welcome-tall.*` for phones (9:16). Each comes as an MP4, a WebM and a poster (`.webp`).

- The film plays muted in the background. **Play with sound** restarts it with narration, and the site opens when it ends.
- **The narration is an AI-generated narrator voice, not Jot's voice.** The narrator introduces Jot ("Meet Jot Punia…") and doesn't pretend to be him. The welcome screen says so.
- The captions are part of the video itself.

Narration script:
> Kia ora, and welcome.
> Meet Jot Punia, your South Auckland agent at Barfoot and Thompson.
> Straight advice, from day one.
> Come on in, and take a look.

## Upgrade: Jot in his own voice
A clip of Jot himself will always beat a narrator. Either:
1. **Phone video.** Jot films himself saying a short welcome: upright, good light, plain background, no one else in shot.
2. **AI avatar in his own voice.** Use HeyGen or Synthesia. Jot records about 2 minutes of himself talking, plus the tool's consent statement, then generates the clip from a script.

Then replace `assets/video/welcome-tall.mp4` (H.264, 9:16, under 4 MB) and its `.webm` and poster. Add a 16:9 cut as `welcome-wide.*`, or copy the tall files over the wide ones. Make sure the captions are in the video. Barfoot & Thompson should see the clip before it goes live, as with any advertising.

## Rules
- Only Jot appears in the film.
- No claims that aren't on the site.
- Never present a synthetic voice as Jot's own unless he recorded it himself (for example, a HeyGen voice clone he made with his own consent).
