    Help Open as app
    Help

    Fair questions. Straight answers.

    Is the appraisal actually free?

    Yes. Free, in writing and backed by comparable local sales. If there aren't good comparables, I'll explain that in writing too. No catch.

    Do I have to dance on camera?

    Only if you want to. Your home is the star. You approve everything before it posts, and nothing is advertised without your OK.

    How often will I hear from you?

    After every open home, and in writing every week. Faster if you text.

    Where can I get advice that isn't yours?

    settled.govt.nz has free, independent guides to buying and selling.

    What if I'm not happy?

    Tell me first and I'll do my best to put it right. You can also use Barfoot & Thompson's complaints process, or contact the Real Estate Authority (REA).
