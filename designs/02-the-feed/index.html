How I sell

Four steps. Zero surprises.

01Appraisal

Get the real number.

A free, written appraisal backed by comparable local sales. No obligation, and no flattering figure just to win your listing.

02Plan and prepare

Plan it like a premiere.

Method of sale, a simple styling game plan and a marketing budget with every cost up front.

Before you sign the agency agreement, I'll recommend you get legal advice.

03Launch

Drop day.

Photos, video and reels go live on barfoot.co.nz, Trade Me and my socials together. After every open home you'll hear who came, what they said and what it means.

04Negotiate and settle

Keys, cheque, done.

I negotiate hard for your best result and stay with you right through to settlement day.
